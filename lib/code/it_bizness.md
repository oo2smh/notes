# Glossary
- `itil`: information tech infrastruture library. an organizational framework to managing it (focusing on providing value to users)
- `value`: perceived benefits, usefulness, or importance of something
- `value stream`: series of steps an organization undertakes to create and deliver products and services to customers
- `service`: means of enabling co-creation by facilitating outcomes that customers want to achieve
- `service offering`: formal description of 1 or more services
- `product`: configuration of an organization's resources designed to offer value for a consumer
- `service management`: a set of specialized organizational capabilities for enabling value for customers in the form of services
---
- `cost`: money spent on activity or service
- `utility`: functionality offered by to meet a particular need (what a service does)
- `warranty`: assurance that a product/service will meet agreed requirements (how the service performs)
- `output`: activity deliverable (tangible or intangible)
- `outcome`: result for a stakeholder enabled by one or more outputs
- `risk`: possible event that could harm or loss, hinder achieving objectives (risk removed/imposed)
---
- `organization`: person/group that has its own functions, responsibilities, authorities to achieve its objectives
- `user`: person that uses services
- `customer`: person defining the service requirements and taking responsibility for the outcomes of using it (aka invested user)
- `sponsor`: person who authorizes the budget for service consumption (money provider, can also be a customer and user as well)
---
- `asset`: any resource or capability that contributes to a delivery of a service
- `event`: any change of state that has significance for the management of a service
- `configuration item`: any component that needs to be managed in order to deliver an IT service
- `change`: addition, modification, or removal of anything that could affect an IT service
- `incident`: an unplanned interruption to a service or a reduction in service utility
- `problem`: cause, potential cause of of one or more incidents
- `known error`: problem that has been analyzed and has a documented cause and a workaround
