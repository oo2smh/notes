# OT Organization [39]
## *_TORAH_ [5]*
  1. Genesis [formation, fall, flood, four patriarchs]
  2. Exodus [domination, liberation, revelation, tabernacle]
  3. Leviticus [sacrifice, santification, laws]
  4. Numbers [census, wanderings, rebellion]
  5. Deuteronomy [reminder (past),  covenant (present), transition (future)]

## *_HISTORY_ [12]*
  1. Joshua [conquest, land, division]
  2. Judges [cycle, sin, oppression, deliverance]
  3. Ruth [ loyalty, tragedy, redemption]
  4. 1/2 Samuel [monarchy, samuel, saul, david]
  5. 1/2 Kings [solomon, division, exile, elijah/elisha]
  6. 1/2 Chronicles [history, davidic kings, worship]
  7. Ezra [return, reconstruction, revival]
  8. Nehemiah [walls, leadership, reform]
  9. Esther [providence, courage, deliverance]

## *_POETRY & WISDOM_ [5]*
  1. Job [suffering, sovereignty, trust]
  2. Psalms [worship, lament, praise]
  3. Proverbs [wisdom, instruction, character]
  4. Ecclesiastes [vanity, search, perspective]
  5. Song of Songs [love, devotion, unity]

## *_PROPHETS_ [17]*
  ### Major [5]
  1. Isaiah [prophecy, judgment, promise]
  2. Jeremiah [warning, weeping, covenant]
  3. Lamentations [sorrow, grief, hope]
  4. Ezekiel [vision, glory, restoration]
  5. Daniel [faith, sovereignty, future]
  ### Minor [12]
  1. Hosea [infidelity, love, restoration]
  2. Joel [judgment, repentance, Spirit]
  3. Amos [justice, righteousenss, accountability]
  4. Obadiah [pride, judgment]
  5. Jonah [mercy, disobedience, forgiveness]
  6. Micah [injustice, humility, restoration]
  7. Nahum [nineveh, wrath, destruction]
  8. Habakkuk [questioning, faith, sovereignty]
  9. Zephaniah [day, judmgment, remnant]
  10. Haggai [rebuilding, priority, temple]
  11. Zechariah [vision, messiah, hope]
  12. Malachi [relationship, repentance, preparation]
